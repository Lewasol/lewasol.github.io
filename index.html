Our story

Lewaheart+Solwarathe sea=Lewasolheart of the sea

Lewasol joins two Tok Pisin words. Together they name the ocean that connects every Pacific island. Every design tells a different province's or nation's story, all joined by one sea.

Our symbol is inspired by the lakatoi, the Motu sailing vessel of the Hiri trade voyages. Its two crab-claw sails form the two halves of one heart, carried by one canoe across one sea.

The logo

One canoe, one heart

Every part of the mark carries meaning.

Two sailsThe heart (lewa), formed from two halves

MastWhat holds the heart together

Canoe hullThe lakatoi and the journey

WaveThe sea (solwara)
