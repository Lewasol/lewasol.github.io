Scams & security

Stay one step ahead of scammers.

Scammers pretend to be banks, businesses and even friends. Learn how NBCL will and won't contact you, how to spot a scam, and what to do if something feels wrong.

Our promise

NBCL will never ask you to…

Share your password or PIN

We will never ask for your Online Banking password, Mobile PIN or card PIN by phone, SMS, email or social media.

Tell us your one-time password

Your OTP is only for you. Anyone who asks you for it, even if they say they're from NBCL, is a scammer.

Move money to a 'safe' account

We will never ask you to transfer money to another account to 'protect' it.

Log in through a link

We won't send you links asking you to log in or update your details. Always type nbcpng.com.pg yourself or use the app.

Know the signs

Common scams in PNG.

Fake bank calls and messagesPhishing and impersonation+
  • Someone calls, texts or messages claiming to be from NBCL and says there's a problem with your account.
  • They create urgency: 'your account will be closed today'.
  • They ask for your password, PIN or OTP, or ask you to move money.
  • What to do: hang up and call us on (+675) 301 5480 using the number on this website or your card.
Fake Facebook pages and WhatsApp accountsSocial media impersonation+
  • Scammers copy our logo and name to create fake pages, ads or WhatsApp profiles.
  • They offer prizes, loans or 'account upgrades' and ask for your details or a fee.
  • What to do: only trust our verified Facebook page. Report fake pages to us and to Facebook.
Prize, lottery and investment scamsToo-good-to-be-true offers+
  • You're told you've won money or can double your savings quickly.
  • You must pay a 'fee' or 'tax' first to receive the winnings.
  • What to do: if it sounds too good to be true, it is. Never pay to receive a prize.
Job, romance and family emergency scamsEmotional pressure+
  • Someone you've met online, or who says they're a relative, urgently needs money.
  • They may ask you to receive and pass on money for them. This could make you part of a crime.
  • What to do: stop, check with family or friends, and never send money to someone you haven't met.
Card skimming and ATM tricksProtect your card+
  • Devices fitted to ATMs or EFTPoS terminals can copy your card details.
  • Someone may watch you enter your PIN or offer to 'help' at the ATM.
  • What to do: cover your PIN, use ATMs in well-lit places, and choose Lokol for extra-secure chip and PIN.

Protect yourself

Simple habits that keep you safe.

  • Keep your password, PIN and OTP private, and don't write them down with your card
  • Use a strong password and change it regularly
  • Turn on the app's alerts and check your account often
  • Keep your phone and the NBCL Mobile App up to date
  • Log out after using Online Banking, especially on shared computers
  • Tell us before you travel overseas

Think you've been scammed?

Act fast. Every minute counts.

  1. Call us straight away on (+675) 301 5480.
  2. Block your card instantly in the Mobile App or Online Banking.
  3. Change your Online Banking password.
  4. Report the scam to the police.
  5. Warn family and friends so they don't fall for it too.
