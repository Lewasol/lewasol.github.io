Questions

Digital banking FAQs

How do I get started?+

Download the free NBCL Mobile App on iOS or Android and register your account in the app, or register for Online Banking in any web browser. You can also register at any branch.

Is the Mobile App free?+

Yes. It is free to download for all NBCL customers.

What can I do in Online Banking?+

View transaction history and real-time balances, download e-Statements, set up scheduled payments, buy and sell foreign currency, and make international payments.

Can I use Online Banking outside PNG?+

Yes, anywhere with an internet connection. If you don't have your PNG SIM, receive your one-time password by email or WhatsApp.

Is digital banking secure?+

Yes. Every login needs a one-time password, and you can block your card instantly in the app. NBCL will never ask for your password or OTP.
